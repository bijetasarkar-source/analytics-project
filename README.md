# Vrinda Store Data Analysis 2022

## Objective
Analyzing the annual sales report of Vrinda Store for the year 2022 and grow sales for the year 2023.

## Dataset
- 31,047 order lines from January to December 2022
- Fields: order ID, customer gender, age and age group, date, order status, sales channel, category, size, quantity, amount (INR), shipping city and state
- Age groups: Teenager (18–29), Adult (30–49), Senior (50+)

## Questions Answered
- Compare sales and orders using a single chart
- Which month had the highest sales and orders?
- Who purchased more in 2022, men or women?
- What are the different order statuses in 2022?
- Which are the top 10 states contributing to sales?
- What is the relationship between age and gender based on the number of orders?
- Which channel contributes the most to sales?
- What is the highest-selling category?

## Key Insights
- **Total sales:** ₹2.12 crore (₹21,176,377) across 31,047 orders.
- **Peak month:** March had the highest sales (₹19.28 lakh) and the most orders (2,819). Sales and orders then trended down through the year, with November and December the lowest.
- **Gender:** Women made 64% of purchases (₹1.36 crore) and men 36% (₹76.1 lakh).
- **Order status:** 92% of orders were delivered (28,641). The rest were Returned (1,045), Cancelled (844) and Refunded (517), about 8% combined.
- **Top states:** Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh lead. The top 10 states contribute about 76% of total sales, and Maharashtra alone about 14%.
- **Age and gender:** Adult women place the most orders (34.6% of all orders), followed by teenage women (21.1%) and senior women (13.7%). In every age group, women order more than men.
- **Top channel:** Amazon contributes the most sales (35.5%), followed by Myntra (23.3%) and Flipkart (21.6%).
- **Top category:** Set is the highest-selling category (₹1.05 crore, about 50% of sales), followed by Kurta and Western Dress.

## Recommendations for 2023
- **Target women shoppers.** They drive most sales and orders, so campaigns and new collections should focus on them, especially adult women.
- **Plan for the sales dip.** Sales fall in the second half of 2022. Run promotions in September to December and prepare stock early for the March peak.
- **Double down on Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart.** Together they bring about 80% of sales. Grow the smaller channels such as Meesho, Ajio and Nalli only where margins justify it.
- **Focus on Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh.** Run regional campaigns there, and test growth in states like West Bengal and Haryana.
- **Push the Set and Kurta categories.** Keep them well stocked, and use Western Dress and Saree as secondary growth lines.
- **Reduce returns, cancellations and refunds.** They account for 8% of orders, so better size guides and product descriptions could help.
